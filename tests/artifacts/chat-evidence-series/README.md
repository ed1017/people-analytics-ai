# Grounded general-chat series

The actual client was bundled and exercised in isolated headless desktop (1440px) and phone (390px) browsers. Synthetic fixtures exercise the real conversation service; no provider or external requests are permitted. The fixture values in these screenshots are test data, not production observations.

Validation: 10 contract/service tests; 20 desktop/phone assertions; 25 existing grounding and plan-presentation regressions; TypeScript, scoped ESLint and production webpack build passed.

Supported: cited headcount snapshots, monthly hires, monthly total/voluntary turnover rates from an already-grounded evidence packet. At least two dated valid observations are required. The compact packet supplies at most three selected observations. Bars are discrete periods, not interpolated lines. Source scope, cutoff, selected-period limits and available synthetic provenance remain visible. A single 6.2% YTD value produces no chart.

The supplemental chart contract is generated after the ordinary answer from verified packet rows, not model-generated numbers. It adds no data reader, provider request, state migration or calculator change. Client charts are bound to the completed request, active evidence context and dataset. They are transient: reloading does not reconstruct charts from text or current data. Reset removes them with their messages.

Not added: new hiring/turnover forecasts, uncertainty intervals, full-history access, or conversion of YTD rates into monthly rates. Existing typed headcount scenarios and Action Plan summary charts remain unchanged. A longer history or additional forecast requires a separately grounded data contract and method; it is not inferred here.
