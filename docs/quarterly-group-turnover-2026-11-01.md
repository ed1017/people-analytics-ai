# Quarterly turnover by group

The reported question "what are turnover rates by group quarter by quarter" previously fell through to the narrow company monthly-rate guard. The new deterministic Home path clarifies whether group means business unit, country or level, then calculates the supported breakdown without an AI provider call. Explicit year and Q1-Q3-style ranges retain their calendar meaning. Existing filters remain in force, including intersections; the answer names its scope. Total turnover is the default, voluntary is explicit. Unsupported dimensions, forecasts and ambiguous periods remain unavailable or ask for clarification.

Source: existing pinned 9037 joint aggregate, SHA256 `bc2cee280389fa646dfdb7901ded66946b4552c65d59d00a0c616bd0cf28d8a8`. The reviewed reader validates all 960 country/BU/level cells, monthly stock-flow identities and company totals. Month-end stocks cover January 2024-September 2026; grouped exit flows cover February 2024-September 2026. No employee records, new synthetic histories or external sources were added. The separate Lab synthetic experiments are not used.

Quarterly rate = sum of the quarter's total or voluntary exits / arithmetic mean of its three closing month-end headcounts x 100. No monthly percentages are summed. No annualization, causal claim or forecast. Complete grouped quarters are Q2 2024-Q3 2026. Q1 2024 lacks January grouped exits; future/incomplete quarters are unavailable. Zero average headcount yields an undefined rate, while known zero exits with positive headcount yield zero. Promotions/transfers are not exits. Group membership follows the existing cell-at-event/month-end contract, not a fixed cohort.

The trusted server binding exposes only scoped monthly aggregates through `turnoverHistory(filters)`, with an explicit `public-group-turnover-history-v1` contract. No HTTP endpoint or public raw-cube access was added. Default dashboard responses, public field allowlist, dataset token/version and binding digest remain byte-identical to the prior release, preserving saved-plan and evidence lineage. The quarterly response has its own hash over source identity, filters and results. Fresh grounding, exact binding/scope checks and existing cancellation limits apply before response; no provider is constructed for this path.

One calculation produces both the answer and the quarter-by-group chart/table. Chart Details expose exit counts, average headcount and unavailable reasons. The supplemental-chart reader recomputes rates from the supplied counts/denominators before accepting them; existing saved-chat persistence is reused. The brief follow-up to the explicit grouping clarification retains metric and year, replacing ambiguous grouping labels. Other conversations retain their existing path.

Validation: focused parser/arithmetic/reader/chart contract tests; actual pinned-source checks across all 960 intersections and group partitions; exact baseline digest/default-response equality; actual Home route test with a throwing provider factory confirms zero provider construction through clarification and supported grouped requests. Independent review identified inclusive-range and multi-dimension clarification defects, corrected before release.
