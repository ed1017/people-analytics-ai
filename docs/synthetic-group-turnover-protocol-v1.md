# Frozen synthetic group turnover experiment

This extends the local workforce generator checkpoint `eef227603a30a679e6977085208d7c061005b088`. It partitions its existing company stock and flows; it does not replace the historical company extract, hiring 90-day start model, satisfaction analysis or product forecast. No person records, external model calls or new operational source boundary are introduced.

The executable protocol is `lib/ml/group-turnover/protocol.json`. Commit it before generating group data or evaluating forecasts. Every numeric generation choice here is a new simulation assumption, not recovered company truth. Four fixed groups start at 4,200 / 4,032 / 159 / 9 people. Group stock must reconcile to company daily stocks, actual hiring starts, voluntary exits, other exits and person-days. Thresholds mask all numeric values when beginning/end headcount is below 50 or a positive monthly voluntary count is below five; recorded zero remains distinct. At least two groups are withheld whenever any are suppressed. These rules exercise small-group behavior, not formal privacy, repeated-release privacy or a production privacy policy.

The existing recent-three-month mean and twelve-month seasonal-naive method definitions remain unchanged, with parity tests against the prior vintage evaluator. Their implementation is adapted to delayed monthly availability: history stops at the last released month, and target calendar months stay fixed. The primary method is the recent mean, fixed in advance. Require at least 24 dense complete released months starting January 2021; internal partial/missing/suppressed periods block rather than disappear. A trailing partial/missing record also blocks. No rate forecast: future exposure is unknown even though generated historical exposure is exact.

Forecast July–September 2026 from a June 30 cutoff. Eleven nonoverlapping earlier target quarters (July 2023–March 2026) calibrate the absolute error of the three-month total, normalized by the square root of its predicted total (minimum one). Each prediction uses only releases available at that earlier quarter-end; calibration outcomes must be available by June 30, 2026. The candidate range uses order statistic `ceil((n+1)*0.9)` with no interpolation and outward integer rounding; all eleven quarters are required. This rolling residual procedure is inspired by conformal scoring; it is **not** a split-conformal coverage guarantee. Temporal exchangeability is not established. See [Oliveira et al., Split Conformal Prediction and Non-Exchangeable Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.15885) for why non-exchangeability needs additional analysis.

Validation uses exactly seeds 1001–1100 for every original family, separate from demo seeds 17, 29, 43. One seeded history supplies one held-out quarter total for each group. A family/group gate requires all 100 outcomes and ranges, empirical coverage at least 90%, and a two-sided 95% Wilson lower bound of at least 90%. Do not pool families or groups to rescue failures, tune the quantile after scoring, or count months/calibration quarters as independent validation samples. These are conditional simulation checks only. Passing does not establish operational coverage or simultaneous guarantees across groups.

Qualification is computed retrospectively at the July 1, 2027 scoring cutoff; it was not available at the June or September 2026 historical forecast origins. Original demo-case predictions and calibration never use validation-case outcomes. Qualification may attach only to the retrospectively assessed July–September demonstration. October–December point forecasts use September 30 releases; that reserved quarter remains unscored and its intervals remain unavailable. A failed or unavailable gate always yields a null published interval, with explicit reasons and visible denominators.

All future truth remains separate from forecast input. All reporting clocks are explicitly simulated. The experiment does not establish new source access, production eligibility, causal effects or individual employment risk. Keep the original synthetic-workforce artifact bundle byte-identical and write this experiment to a separate evidence directory.
