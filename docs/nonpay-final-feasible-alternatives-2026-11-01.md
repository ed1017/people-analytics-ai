## Australia: original-file transfer, not access denial

The [official OSL catalogue](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/data/occupation-shortage) offers the six-digit workbook, a separate four-digit workbook, aggregate tables, findings and methodology reports. No equivalent CSV, JSON or second download mechanism for the six-digit records was listed. The interactive page exposed a loading error rather than occupation ratings. Readable reports contain aggregate findings and definitions, not the target records.

The distinct four-digit workbook was also assessed. It could support an explicitly labelled occupation-group reference: ANZSCO 2247 may fit substantive management-consulting duties, and 2713 may fit qualified legal advisory/drafting duties. It is not a substitute for six-digit or privacy-specific claims. The exact workbook classification vintage, national ratings, confidence labels and notes still need verification. Title alone does not confirm primary duties or professional credentials.

The four-digit direct retrieval timed out after 25 seconds with HTTP 000 and zero bytes. The earlier six-digit request timed out after 40 seconds with zero bytes. Web-tool unsupported Excel MIME responses are tool limitations, not denials. No unchanged six-digit retry, alternate proxy, account or private API was used. Neither original was downloaded or hashed.

**Minimum input:** unchanged official workbook(s), including all worksheets and notes:

- `2026_occupation_shortage_list_-_6_digit_anzsco_and_osca.xlsx` — advertised 151,746 bytes; research URL: `https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-10/2026_occupation_shortage_list_-_6_digit_anzsco_and_osca.xlsx`.
- `2026_unit_group_shortage_list_-_4_digit_anzsco.xlsx` — advertised 81,658 bytes; research URL: `https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-10/2026_unit_group_shortage_list_-_4_digit_anzsco.xlsx`.

JSA's general CC BY 4.0 terms have third-party and other exceptions. Original notes and exact source ownership must be checked before import. Recovering a file does not automatically qualify all nine Australian vacancies.

- The [World Bank occupational-mobility reproducibility catalogue](https://reproducibility.worldbank.org/catalog/543) distinguishes licensed US O*NET inputs from an Indian green-jobs qualification database. Neither establishes the required local company-role evidence with verified applicable rights. No package was imported.

**Minimum input:** an exact occupational/qualification original with applicable reuse/adaptation terms, ownership, current approval/version and date. Existing DGE/NQR rights questions and the NSDC access denial remain separate. No paid registration, API key, permission request or new account was used.
