# Home recommendations as Action Plans

For substantive workforce or business decisions, Home now requests a short conditional recommendation, brief supporting information, several useful plans, then optional targeted questions before selection. Three is an initial presentation target, not a permanent cap. A useful fourth alternative or combination can fit the existing four-reference final response; more proposals can follow in chat. Brief definitions and focused refinements do not require a fresh set of plans.

The existing questions field holds up to two optional questions. They follow the plans in the answer and appear before review cards. There is no new form or answer requirement: a user may answer in chat for new/revised plans, or skip directly to reviewing and choosing. Questions address facts that could change the recommendation; supplied answers are not requested again. Optional further reading/investigation remains after proposals and uses verified links or explicit suggested checks, never invented sources.

An answer that changes a plan is evaluated as a new revision with the same candidate ID and exact source references. A new alternative or combination gets a new ID. Partial answers preserve unrelated assumptions and the original objective/turn; unanswered costs, capacity, readiness, overlap and causal benefits remain unknown. A chat preference changes only conversational focus. The existing explicit Choose control saves the reviewed goal and attaches that proposal; operational Apply is separate.

The batch tool still handles at most three candidates. Final replies still reference at most four checked candidates; working history still retains at most twelve revisions with ancestry guards. Additional plans must fit the existing four model rounds, six operations, 5,000 output tokens, request/result limits and no-retry policy. No contract version, calculator or model policy was expanded. A bound can stop further work; it never silently discards needed provenance or raises a paid-call limit. Three original plans plus a sourced hybrid fit in three model rounds. One optional playbook read plus those evaluations fit in four under compact fixtures.

The [SWP reasoning playbook](swp-reasoning-playbook.md) provides small source-attributed method sections on demand, supply, constrained options, resource accounting and delivery. The model requests only relevant sections through a bounded local read; ordinary answers need not load them. It does not introduce a mandatory questionnaire or workforce evidence.

## Local verification and limits

Unit and actual-route regressions cover four initial plans, a later fifth plan, combinations, partial answers, changed recommendations, unchanged unknowns, deliberate saving with unanswered questions and unchanged budgets. Desktop/mobile actual-client checks cover optional questions, revision lineage, Review navigation, skipping to Choose, reload and prior saved-data preservation. Framework cases exercise hiring freezes, fixed budget with growth, skill mismatch, delayed unproven automation and duplicate resource/cost exclusions through existing calculation contracts.

Responses in these tests are scripted synthetic fixtures. They verify application behavior, guards and arithmetic; they do not establish that a live model follows the guidance, asks useful questions or recommends strong interventions. Independent source review and a separately scoped live semantic acceptance remain necessary before publication. The frozen 8bd365e app and 7dc2a19/4c41533 acceptance sources remain untouched. The separate local diagnostic successor remains bound to the old app; it is not acceptance of this changed tool catalog or flow. No provider run, reservation, deployment or release is part of this change.
