# Modeled position duties

Status: approved implementation in progress; not released.

## Approved scope and provenance

The user approved fictional-company duty descriptions for `IT-ENG`, `CORPDEV-ANA`, `CSM-GEN` and `PROD-PM` on October 31. These four roles cover five modeled US vacancies. This authorizes transparent planning templates, not assertions of observed employee work, authenticated job descriptions or verified Finance approvals.

| Role | Modeled purpose | External mapping decision |
|---|---|---|
| IT Engineer | Enterprise systems integration, configuration, testing and service reliability | No default network-architect match. Systems-analyst applicability needs substantive requirements/design work, not support alone. |
| Corporate Development Analyst | Acquisition screening, valuation, due diligence and transaction risk | Do not substitute Management Analysts process-improvement evidence for M&A work. |
| Customer Success Specialist | Onboarding, adoption, support coordination and customer feedback | Do not assume sales quota, closing or renewals revenue ownership to justify service-sales evidence. |
| Product Manager | Customer discovery, product priorities, requirements and outcome measurement | Do not substitute General Operations Manager or project scheduling for product management. |

The templates do not expand external reference coverage: 49/197 non-pay and 59/197 pay remain unchanged. Duties follow the fictional business roles; roles are not rewritten to increase source matches. No Lab changes, paid calls or new data access.

## Version and adoption contract

Templates live in a separate immutable versioned fixture. Each has a stable role code, exact display role, country scope, duties, exclusions and `fictional-planning-assumption` provenance. Existing position IDs, inventory versions, costs, funding, readiness and allocations remain unchanged.

Adoption is explicit and bound to dataset, goal, saved scenario, allocation revision/signature and template identity/version. Review and cancel do not mutate saved work; confirmation appends a duty revision. A changed store or selection invalidates the review. Reload never automatically chooses the latest duty revision. Explicit reopen/clear controls which adopted assumptions travel with an explanation.

The browser sends only a pinned record; the server resolves canonical template content. Browser consent is not authenticated saved-state proof. Legacy explanation packets without modeled duties remain supported. Backup and tracking-goal copies preserve exact versions and bind only to their corresponding saved allocations. No external reference or pay assumption is automatically attached.
