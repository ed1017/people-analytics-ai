# Dormant SWP planning reference

This application guidance adapts public workforce-planning principles to a fictional professional-services business. It is original wording, not an official framework implementation, reproduced checklist, certification or endorsement. It is retained as design reference material. The active Home model does not receive these sections through a playbook tool.

The [CIPD workforce-planning standards](https://www.cipd.org/uk/the-people-profession/the-profession-map/explore-the-profession-map/specialist-knowledge/workforce-planning/) inform dynamic scenarios and the mix and sequencing of interventions across workforce dimensions. [Skills for Health's public Six Steps overview](https://www.skillsforhealth.org.uk/integrated-solutions/workforce-development/six-step-methodology/) informs the iterative connection between service needs, workforce demand, available capability, action and review. The [OPM model](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/human-capital-framework/reference-materials/strategic-alignment/workforceplanning.pdf) reinforces alignment, skill-gap analysis, implementation responsibilities and adjustment against progress.

Public sources were consulted on 2026-10-09. The supplied [Skills for Health PDF address](https://www.skillsforhealth.org.uk/app/uploads/2023/07/The-Six-Steps-Methodology-Checklist.pdf) initially returned indexed excerpts, but subsequent direct reads returned 404. Attribution in the stored reference therefore uses the accessible official overview. The current checklist page offers a request form; no form, paid service or member material was accessed. No PDF is included in the repository.

## Application-specific sections

- **Demand:** Translate the business outcome into scoped work, units, effort, periods, service requirements and conditional demand. Separate won work from pipeline and account for current commitments.
- **Supply:** Examine skills, proficiency, actual time available, management span/bandwidth, release, backfill and ramp/training lead times. Employee count alone does not prove capability or capacity.
- **Options:** Compare appropriate hiring, development, redeployment, contracting, automation and reprioritisation, including sequences. Respect freeze/budget/timing constraints. A delayed automation benefit cannot fill an earlier gap.
- **Resources:** Existing code owns supported numerical calculations. Keep cash, staff hours and capacity distinct; preserve unknown totals and prevent duplicate people, fees or savings across paths. Frameworks do not prove readiness or causal effects.
- **Delivery:** Identify proposed owners, first actions, milestones, risks and measures. Review scoped baseline and progress, then revise assumptions and recommendations without overwriting history or automatically saving anything.

These detailed guardrails are application design choices requested for this product. Source frameworks provide planning structure, not company observations, staffing ratios, delivery guarantees or measured benefit estimates. Healthcare service language is adapted to client work, service quality and business delivery; clinical staffing rules are not transferred.

## Selective use and calculation boundary

`readSwpPlaybook` remains an offline reference helper returning one or two distinct sections and their relevant source links, without network or database access. The active Home tool catalog does not expose `read_workforce_planning_playbook`, and section text is absent from the permanent prompt. This release therefore does not claim model access to the reference. Source-attributed planning support remains an unfinished requirement to review separately; restoring a tool is not part of the focused release cleanup.

Demand/staffing tools calculate only their existing supported formulas, with explicit units, periods, provenance and uncertainty. Contracting and automation can be proposed qualitatively; this release adds no calculator for their claimed benefits. The playbook cannot create a new numeric field, infer free capacity from headcount, establish shared people or expenses, or turn a hiring freeze into permission to contract. Review/save and progress recording remain explicit user actions.

Tests use synthetic inputs to exercise constraints, code arithmetic, unchanged unknowns, lineage and interaction. They do not independently validate AI recommendations or prove any real-world workforce effect. Later semantic acceptance should review usefulness, optional-question relevance, changing recommendations, feasible versus unknown tradeoffs and unsupported quantitative claims against actual tool receipts. Its exact source, scope and spend require separate coordination.
