## Scope

Connect selected company-register US software vacancies to the existing national Software Developers wage reference. A visitor reviews the occupation mapping, annual full-time payroll basis and employer-cost allowance before explicitly applying it to the current position scenario. Budget, hiring fees, timing, allocation and Finance approval state are not inferred from market data.

Existing location comparisons remain available as references. Country-only inventory does not establish a New York state or metro match. UK occupation-group hiring difficulty, Canadian vacancies and employment totals do not become role-level hiring time, applicant availability or training readiness.

## Saved-work contract

Keep the existing strict allocation input and result schema. A separate versioned field binds adopted evidence to the exact saved allocation revision, including source, statistic, mapped role, selected position IDs, cost assumptions and signature. Changed cost or selected positions invalidate the current association without changing historical records. Save/reopen, backup validation and the explicit General exploration-to-tracking-goal handoff must retain the relationship.
