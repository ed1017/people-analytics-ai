## Limits that prevent calling this a unified replacement history

Matching aggregate margins does not recover missing joins or chronology. **Time-to-fill, open age, source/recruiter/BU/role allocations, paired source dates and monthly interview activity are not calibrated.** The residual open pool intentionally starts in October 2023 to make the aggregate construction feasible; its age distribution therefore differs materially from the source's median 83 days, average 83.1 days and 323 over-60-day reqs. Its generated fill timing also does not reproduce source median 49 / average 51.7 days. It must not drive those cards or planning timing.

The v2 proposal does not yet emit activated forecasts. Before activation, its generated stock series needs review and count-baseline validation, particularly the last-month effect of constructing only observed-through-cutoff fills. Do not reuse v1's 112-count forecasts, manufacture future openings, or infer future hiring throughput from active stock. True source gaps remain unknown; no gaps are concealed by claiming this generated ledger is measured history.
