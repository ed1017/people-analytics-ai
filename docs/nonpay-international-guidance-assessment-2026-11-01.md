# International occupational guidance feasibility — 2026-11-01

Assessment only; no mapping or application changes.

ISCO-08 is a genuine global classification and could provide separate qualitative occupational context where the actual primary duties match. It is not a country-specific demand, pay, skills-rating, available-candidate or hiring-time source. Its 436 unit groups are broader than many modern app role titles. The current ILO site identifies ISCO-08 as current while revision is underway. Skill levels are classification dimensions, not employee proficiency or app grade equivalence.

Original sources checked:
- https://isco.ilo.org/en/isco-08/ — international scope, hierarchy and unit-group definition.
- https://isco.ilo.org/en/faq/ — ISCO can be used without prior authorization; current version and uses.
- https://www.ilo.org/ilostat-files/ISCO/newdocs-08-2021/ISCO-08/ISCO-08%20EN%20Vol%201.pdf — original 2012 publication, group definitions and occupation inclusions/exclusions. Publicly accessible through the official site's download link. Browser code list returned an empty client-rendered shell, not a denial.
- https://www.ilo.org/rights-and-permissions — post-May 3, 2023 open-access policy does not automatically license pre-2023 publications or datasets.

Rights boundary: general classification use is expressly permitted by the current ISCO FAQ. The original 2012 PDF's copyright page permits attributed short excerpts but refers broader reproduction/translation to permission. Its 2021 URL does not turn it into a post-2023 CC-BY publication. Do not treat the FAQ as a verified bulk text/adaptation licence. Before copying complete duty lists into a distributed fixture, resolve the exact downloadable dataset/text reuse terms or obtain relevant permission. No contact, account or permission request was made here.

Granularity examples: 2512 Software Developers and 2521 Database Designers and Administrators are genuine unit groups, potentially useful for actual software-development or database-design work after duty review. A BI Analyst, HR Systems Specialist, cybersecurity specialist or AI role is not automatically interchangeable with these groups. A minor group or residual category is not a fallback merely to reach a coverage target. Existing role duties remain unchanged; no per-position mappings were established in this assessment.

Coverage decision required: introduce a separate global occupational-guidance scope and independently confirmed primary-duty match, with source vintage and classification provenance. Preserve the country-specific evidence status and its existing counts. If the product adds an 'any relevant external context' count, display it separately from local evidence coverage; do not silently redefine the current 197-position target. Global guidance can contextualize India, Singapore or Brazil positions without claiming to describe those labor markets. Their source-access/rights/local-measurement gaps remain open.

Smallest defensible next step if approved: a single exact-duty-fit, link-first global reference under a distinct contract, or a short attributed source excerpt within confirmed reuse terms. Full task-list ingestion is not ready on the currently verified rights evidence. No app changes, numeric estimates or coverage gains recommended by this research alone.
