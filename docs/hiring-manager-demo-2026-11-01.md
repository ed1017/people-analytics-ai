## Repeatable reader path

1. Start a fresh General exploration. Use Talent inventory (expand desktop navigation, or Browse pages on mobile). Select the two US Frontend Software Engineer vacancies across Digital Services and Sales: `MODEL2-BU-DIGITAL--SWE-FRONT--L7--0001` and `MODEL2-BU-SALES--SWE-FRONT--L7--0002`. They are not one team.
2. Set four months and an illustrative incremental cash allowance of $100,000. Enter annual hired-FTE cost $163,176, hire fee $6,000, repurpose cost $2,000, training fee $2,500, training effort 80 hours at $50/hour, no backfill. Hire arrival is month 2 and readiness month 4; training readiness month 4; redeployment month 1. Source team Client Operations, assumed release 1 FTE for internal routes.
3. Save three alternatives: hire 2; hire 1/train 1; hire 1/redeploy 1. Explicitly select **redeploy** for the third: redesign keeps a vacancy unstaffed. Compare alternatives pairwise with A/B. Confirm both selected-vacancy capacity and company-wide net capacity, donor gap, readiness assumptions and separate employee-time value.
4. Reopen the third saved scenario and save a five-month revision. The original four-month revision must remain unchanged. Use the explicit tracking-goal bridge; preview/cancel first, then confirm. Repeated confirmation must not duplicate the goal or erase General exploration.
5. Track a manually entered 1.5 FTE result against the 2 FTE selected-vacancy target. Review should show 0.5 FTE below target. Reload and navigate Back/Forward. Download a native backup, preview/cancel restore, then confirm restore; preserve saved revisions and selections.
6. Consult Skills Intelligence as aggregate company context. It does not identify a suitable releasable employee. Obtain actual employee/release evidence before executing an internal move. A saved-scenario explanation uses its explicit selected revision; unrelated general chat is not proof that the same plan is selected.
