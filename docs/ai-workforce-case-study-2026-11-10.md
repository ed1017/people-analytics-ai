# AI workforce transition case study

The `/case-study` page uses dated Accenture publications as industry context and keeps Ed Inc.'s existing fictional workforce data unchanged. It does not identify real or synthetic employees for action. An explicit planning overlay asks how to staff ten AI implementation analyst roles while a fictional legacy managed-services workstream contracts. The contracting workstream, role requirement, internal pools, costs, timing and release are scenario assumptions, not findings from Accenture or the app's workforce dataset.

The shared fixed-role staffing calculator compares three declared strategies over six months: hire ten (USD 600,000 listed cash), redeploy three and hire seven (USD 456,000), or develop four, redeploy three and hire three (USD 276,000). At the initial three-month target and USD 500,000 budget, the second strategy is the conditional shortlist. At a four-month target, the third becomes eligible. With no release or a tighter budget/deadline, the page returns an appropriate blocked or empty result. This is not a search for the globally optimal mix.

All costs and pools are invented. Listed cash excludes existing internal payroll and unentered costs; complete cost and operational feasibility remain unverified. No realized savings, training effectiveness, proficiency, ROI or day-one coverage is claimed. Full-readiness timing, whole-period cash and entered training hours remain distinct. The case uses no provider calls and does not import, save or change a plan. About links to the new page; the existing guided save-and-revisit service-team example remains separate.

Primary context: Accenture's June 13, 2023 AI investment announcement and FY2025 Form 10-K. The 80,000 AI-talent figure is a goal from the 2023 announcement, while approximately 77,000 AI/data practitioners is the FY2025 reported figure. Q4 FY2025 workforce rebalancing included upskilling, exits where reskilling was not viable, and efficiencies including AI. Total workforce increased year over year; the fictional workstream contraction is not an Accenture business finding. These facts do not parameterize the calculator.
